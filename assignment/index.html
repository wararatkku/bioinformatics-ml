Breast Cancer Wisconsin
After Class • Mini Research Extension

Assignment

Extend the classroom experiment and communicate what the evidence means.

Choose ONE extension

Level 1 — Model Improvement

Add one classifier (e.g., SVM, k-NN or Gradient Boosting), evaluate it fairly and compare it with the classroom models.

Level 2 — Feature Selection

Train with all features and then with a selected subset. Explain whether performance and interpretability change.

Level 3 — Research Challenge

Use stratified cross-validation. Report mean F1 ± standard deviation and discuss model stability.

Required submission

  • Completed Jupyter Notebook
  • Model comparison table
  • Top features / interpretation
  • Limitations and next step

Assessment — 10 points

Criterion Points
Pre-class readiness 1
Data exploration & preprocessing 2
ML implementation 2
Model evaluation 2
Interpretation & limitations 2
Important: The highest accuracy does not automatically receive the highest score. Your reasoning, evaluation design, interpretation and reproducibility matter.
